Our Foundation

School Philosophy

“Teach ye all nations.” — Matthew 28:19

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. believes that authentic Catholic education forms socially conscious, morally upright, and intellectually competent individuals who live out the Gospel in their daily lives.

As an educational arm of the Diocese of Kabankalan, KCC actively participates in the Church’s mission of evangelization by integrating faith, culture, and life across every academic discipline and co-curricular program.

Rooted in the preferential option for the poor, the College strives to make quality Catholic education accessible and inclusive, empowering students from all walks of life to achieve their God-given potential.

Through academic excellence anchored on a solid moral foundation, KCC prepares graduates to become servant leaders and effective agents of societal transformation.

Direction & Purpose

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

A Christ-Centered Learning Community

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. envisions a Christ-centered learning community recognized for academic excellence, holistic faith formation, innovative research, and compassionate service in the Diocese of Kabankalan and beyond.

Our Mission

Quality & Inclusive Catholic Education

KCC commits to provide quality and inclusive Catholic education, rooted in the Gospel and driven by innovative research and meaningful community engagements, empowering its members to become effective agents of societal transformation.

Since 1927

A Brief History of Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc.

Nearly a century of faith, resilience, and educational leadership in southern Negros Occidental.

1927

Founding of Kabankalan Institute

Established as Kabankalan Institute, laying the groundwork for secondary education in Kabankalan and neighboring towns.

1940–1946

First Commencement & War Years

Celebrated its first commencement exercises before weathering the challenges of World War II and reopening to rebuild the community.

1953

Columban Fathers Acquire the Academy

The Missionary Society of St. Columban acquired the institution, strengthening its Catholic identity and academic standards.

Columban Era

Growth of Catholic Formation

Under the dedicated stewardship of the Columban Fathers, enrollment expanded and campus life flourished with strong faith programs.

1963–1964

College & Elementary Departments Open

Expanded into higher education and elementary schooling, becoming a comprehensive Catholic educational institution.

1983

Diocesan Administration

Administration transitioned to the local Church, deepening KCC’s alignment with diocesan pastoral and educational priorities.

1987

The Great Fire & Reconstruction

Following a devastating fire, the KCC community united in faith and determination to reconstruct stronger, modern campus facilities.

Campus Expansion

St. Columban Arts Center

New academic and cultural facilities, including the St. Columban Arts Center, were built to support student formation and arts.

1988

New Diocese & Good Shepherd

With the creation of the Diocese of Kabankalan, KCC became a cornerstone of Catholic education in southern Negros.

1996

Partnership with the Carmelite Sisters

The Carmelite Sisters joined in nurturing the spiritual and academic life of the college community.

2003–2011

Bishop Buzon & Talubangi Tech Center

Under Bishop Patricio A. Buzon, SDB, KCC expanded its technical and academic reach, including the Talubangi Tech Center.

2018–2019

Bishop Galbines & Fr. Lucerna Leadership

Under Bishop Louie P. Galbines and Fr. Lucerna, KCC strengthened academic quality, facilities, and institutional accreditation.

2025

Fr. Richard L. Lachica, MAEd Appointed President

Marking a new chapter as KCC approaches its centennial in 2027 with renewed commitment to synodality, innovation, and excellence.

The Emblem, Decoded

The School Seal

The Twelve Waves

Represent the Twelve Apostles and the universal mission of the Church sent forth to proclaim the Good News.

The Motto

“Teach Ye All Nations” (Matthew 28:19), the Great Commission guiding KCC’s educational ministry.

The Two Stars

Symbolize faith and reason illuminating the path of every KCCian learner.

The Philippine Map

Signifies KCC’s commitment to nation-building and service to the Filipino people.

Dark Blue & Gold

Dark blue reflects Marian devotion, depth of wisdom, and steadfast loyalty; gold represents excellence, nobility of purpose, and the light of Christ.

Who We Are

Core Values: K-C-C-I-A-N-S

Seven guiding values lived out every day by the Kabankalan Catholic College community.

K

Kindness

Showing genuine compassion, respect, and charity toward every member of the community.

C

Christ-Centeredness

Placing Christ and the Gospel at the heart of learning, leadership, and daily life.

C

Creativity

Cultivating curiosity, innovation, and thoughtful problem-solving in service of others.

I

Integrity

Upholding honesty, moral courage, and consistency between faith and action.

A

Accountability

Taking responsible stewardship of our talents, duties, and commitments to society.

N

Nurturing

Fostering a safe, supportive, and encouraging environment where every learner thrives.

S

Synodality

Walking together in communion, participation, and mission as one Catholic family.
