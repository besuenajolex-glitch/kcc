Basic Education College Programs
Academic Track

Academic Strands

Designed for learners planning to pursue university degrees across science, business, humanities, and interdisciplinary fields.

STEM

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics

Strengthens analytical thinking, scientific inquiry, and mathematical modeling for technical and health-related careers.

Key Subjects: Pre-Calculus, Basic Calculus, General Biology, General Chemistry, General Physics
College Paths: Engineering, Information Technology, Nursing, Medicine, Architecture, Natural Sciences
ABM

Accountancy, Business, and Management

Builds foundational skills in financial management, entrepreneurship, economics, and organizational leadership.

Key Subjects: Fundamentals of ABM, Business Math, Applied Economics, Business Finance, Principles of Marketing
College Paths: Accountancy, Business Administration, Management Accounting, Tourism & Hospitality
HUMSS

Humanities and Social Sciences

Focuses on communication, society, politics, culture, and human behavior for service-oriented professions.

Key Subjects: Creative Writing, Philippine Politics & Governance, World Religions, Disciplines in Social Sciences
College Paths: Education, Psychology, Criminology, Philosophy, Law, Communication, Social Work
GAS

General Academic Strand

A flexible, well-rounded strand for students exploring multiple disciplines before choosing a college major.

Key Subjects: Humanities, Social Sciences, Organization & Management, Applied Economics, Electives
College Paths: Open pathway to Education, Business, Liberal Arts, and Interdisciplinary Degrees
Technical-Vocational-Livelihood

Technical Professional (TVL) Track

Hands-on skills training preparing graduates for national certification (TESDA), employment, entrepreneurship, or further studies.

TVL · ICT

Information and Communications Technology

Practical training in computer systems servicing, programming fundamentals, productivity tools, and digital media.

TVL · HE

Home Economics

Develops industry-ready skills in culinary arts, bread and pastry production, food & beverage services, and hospitality.

TVL · IA

Industrial Arts

Hands-on technical competence in electrical installation, maintenance, drafting, and applied trade skills.
