About Us

A Legacy of Faith & Education

Founded in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College has served as the home of Catholic education in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental. Guided by the Gospel and rooted in the mission of the Diocese of Kabankalan, KCC forms generations of learners who excel academically, live with integrity, and serve their communities with compassion.

From Kindergarten through College, our holistic approach blends rigorous instruction with spiritual formation, research, and meaningful community engagement.
