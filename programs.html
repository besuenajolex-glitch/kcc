College Department

College of Management, Accountancy and Technology

BSBA

Bachelor of Science in Business Administration

Develops strategic business thinkers ready for corporate management, banking, and entrepreneurship.

Majors: Financial Management, Marketing Management
Career Paths: Financial Analyst, Marketing Manager, Business Consultant, Entrepreneur
BSIT

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology

Equips students with software engineering, networking, database, and cybersecurity competencies.

Career Paths: Software Developer, Systems Analyst, Network Administrator, IT Project Manager
BSA

Bachelor of Science in Accountancy

Comprehensive preparation in financial accounting, auditing, taxation, and CPA licensure readiness.

Career Paths: Certified Public Accountant, External/Internal Auditor, Tax Specialist, Chief Finance Officer
BSAIS

Bachelor of Science in Accounting Information System

Combines accounting principles with enterprise information systems and business analytics.

Career Paths: Accounting Systems Analyst, ERP Specialist, Risk & Compliance Officer, IT Auditor
BSMA

Bachelor of Science in Management Accounting

Focuses on cost accounting, budgeting, performance evaluation, and strategic financial decision-making.

Career Paths: Management Accountant, Budget Analyst, Corporate Controller, Financial Planner
BSTM

Bachelor of Science in Tourism Management

Prepares service-oriented leaders for hospitality, travel operations, events, and sustainable tourism.

Career Paths: Tourism Officer, Travel Operations Manager, Events Planner, Airline & Hospitality Specialist

College of Education, Arts and Sciences

BEEd

Bachelor of Elementary Education

Forms compassionate and skilled educators dedicated to foundational childhood learning.

Major: General Education
Career Paths: Elementary School Teacher, Curriculum Developer, Academic Coordinator
BSEd

Bachelor of Secondary Education

Trains subject-matter educators ready to inspire junior and senior high school learners.

Majors: English, Filipino, Values Education, Science, Mathematics
Career Paths: High School Teacher, Subject Area Coordinator, Instructional Designer
BECEd

Bachelor of Early Childhood Education

Specializes in early childhood development, play-based pedagogy, and preschool instruction.

Career Paths: Preschool Teacher, Early Childhood Specialist, Learning Center Director
BPEd

Bachelor of Physical Education

Builds expertise in physical education, sports science, coaching, and wellness promotion.

Career Paths: PE Teacher, Athletic Coach, Sports Program Coordinator, Wellness Trainer
AB Philosophy

Bachelor of Arts in Philosophy

Cultivates critical inquiry, ethics, logic, and strong preparation for law, ministry, and public service.

Career Paths: Pre-Law & Legal Research, Educator, Ethics Consultant, Pastoral & Public Service
BS Psychology

Bachelor of Science in Psychology

Explores human behavior, mental health, psychological assessment, and organizational psychology.

Career Paths: Psychometrician, HR Specialist, Guidance Associate, Pre-Medicine / Graduate Studies

Bachelor of Science in Criminology

Kabankalan Catholic College
Est. 1927 · Negros Occidental

A Catholic school in Kabankalan City forming students who are grounded in faith, strong in academics, and ready to serve.
