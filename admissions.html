Admissions

Everything you need to know about enrolling at KCC — steps, requirements, and what to expect.

The Process

Four Steps to Enrollment

Follow these simple steps to begin your journey as a KCCian.

01

Submit Requirements

Complete the online pre-registration via the KCC Wela portal or submit your admission documents to the Registrar’s Office.

02

Entrance Examination

Take the scheduled KCC Admission & Placement Examination to assess academic readiness and guide strand or course placement.

03

Interview

Meet with the Department Head, Dean, or Guidance Counselor to discuss your goals, program choice, and student life at KCC.

04

Enrollment

Finalize subject enlistment, settle assessment fees at the Finance Office, and receive your official registration form and ID schedule.

Admission Requirements

K to Grade 10

Basic Education

  • Original Report Card (Form 138 / SF9)
  • PSA Birth Certificate (photocopy)
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • Baptismal Certificate (for Catholic applicants)
  • 2x2 ID Pictures (white background)
Grades 11 & 12

Senior High School

  • Grade 10 Report Card (SF9)
  • Junior High School Completion Certificate
  • PSA Birth Certificate (photocopy)
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • ESC / QVR Voucher Certificate (if applicable)
Tertiary

College Applicants

  • Grade 12 Report Card (Form 138) or Honorable Dismissal (transferees)
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • PSA Birth Certificate (photocopy)
  • Official Transcript of Records (for transferees)
  • 2x2 ID Pictures (white background)
